Mythic starters: three options per character

Proposals only. Nothing in the game uses these yet; champions #1–#5 and every player's save are unchanged. Each character has its artist's sheet on the left (the original file, untouched) and options A, B and C on the right. Every head is the artist's own drawing, traced from the sheet in its four views and set on the game's kit body. Each fills exactly the same head box as #1–#5, width and height, and the last tile shows it on the pitch beside #1 with the hitbox dashed over both: every champion's hitbox is identical.

Chosen (Idan, 2026-10-08): A for all four. They are in the game as the Mythic cards (tools/chars/mythic_build.py); B and C stay here for comparison.

Face, hair, kit and accessories are the artist's in all three options, drawn as a cartoon (the artist's line work over flat colours); they differ in how flat, and in the finish. Pick one letter per character.
