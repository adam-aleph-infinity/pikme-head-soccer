# 45 champions → 45 Head Soccer power shots (spec)

_Research + spec only; no game code reads this yet. Data: `docs/hs-45-powers.json` (checked by `test-hs-45-powers.mjs`)._

Idan's decision: every champion gets its **own** power, copied from one real Head Soccer character — same flight,
same look, same effect on the defender, drawn in our own canvas art. The 11 families in `shared/hs-powers.js`
become building blocks (`engine` column).

## How the order was chosen

Strictly escalating: **stage 1 weakest → stage 45 hardest and flashiest.** Score = `stars×100 + defend×10 + spectacle`,
non-decreasing from 1 to 45 (the test checks it).

- **stars** — HS Arcade rating [A]: the first nine HS characters are 0.5…4.5 (Korea, Cameroon, Nigeria, USA, Japan,
  Russia, Argentina, Italy, Brazil); every later character is 5. So stars fix stages 1–9, and the 36 five-star
  characters are ordered by the next two keys.
- **defend** (1–9) — how hard it is to stop: 1 plain straight · 2 straight + ailment · 3 ground · 4 downward/dipping ·
  5 delay / hidden / bobbing · 6 decoys or a field hazard · 7 several real balls, grab, or a block that is punished ·
  8 multi-stage (hazard + ball, pinning decoys, only-one-counterable) · 9 cannot be countered / critical cutscene / summon.
- **spectacle** (0–9) — how big the look is, from the plain comet (0) to screen-sized summons and cutscenes (9).

**Known dip, flagged for Idan:** because stars come first, Brazil (4.5★, phoenix + reversed controls) sits at stage 9
above Valentine (5★, slow straight + toy) at stage 10. The wiki itself says the stars are "obsolete as a measure of
difficulty" [A]. If Idan prefers pure difficulty, move Brazil to ~stage 20 (among the downward shots) and Italy to ~12.

**Title clashes:** the Hebrew titles stay per stage, so most themes no longer match (`titleClash`). Good fits: 25 המטעה = magic,
32 הרוזן האפל = dark hand, 38 המשגר = launcher, 45 אדון הזמן = cosmic gauntlet. Better homes exist for some titles
(e.g. איש השלג (20) fits Canada/Switzerland). Renaming is out of scope here.

## Why each is harder than the one before

1. **South Korea** — First stage: the plain straight comet, the easiest shot in HS to stop.
2. **Cameroon** — Same easy straight line, but a missed block leaves the defender shocked (slow, no jump).
3. **Nigeria** — Ground shot: no header block, reaches jumpers, and a hit costs 3 s unconscious.
4. **USA** — First decoy shot: eight balls then an invisible one, the defender must guess.
5. **Japan** — Five streaks at five heights from above with a hidden real ball: more to read than USA.
6. **Russia** — A dipping path that also freezes the blocker: blocking it is punished, not only missing it.
7. **Argentina** — First delay: the dragon baits an early jump and the ball arrives a second later.
8. **Italy** — Four stars in HS: the big ball cannot be headed and drags the defender in.
9. **Brazil** — Highest star rating below 5: rise-then-dive plus reversed controls.
10. **Valentine** — First 5-star: straight again but the hit turns you into a kickable toy.
11. **Poland** — Straight like Valentine but the blocker, not just the hit player, is frozen and launched.
12. **Turkey** — Adds a hidden ball and a short delay to the straight line.
13. **Super Saiyan** — First downward shot of the 5-stars: a big ball from above that is hard to head away.
14. **Portugal** — Downward and a hit hangs you out of play.
15. **Canada** — Two shot variants to read, and a hit becomes a snowman.
16. **China** — Two very different flights plus a 2 s removal from play.
17. **France** — The ball travels unseen under every obstacle and pops up behind you.
18. **Chile** — Downward shot that also drags you into your goal.
19. **Egypt** — A stall that breaks jump timing, and a kickable-statue hit.
20. **Devil** — First bobbing path: the ball height keeps changing.
21. **The Netherlands** — Slow but devastating: a hit removes you for 3 s.
22. **New Zealand** — Adds a field hazard before the shot: dodge the lightning, then deal with the ball.
23. **Sweden** — Decoys that hurt and a high/low guess.
24. **Cyborg** — Two shot states and a missile volley with one real ball.
25. **Australia** — Air/ground variants, a growing ball and a 5 s transform.
26. **Czech Republic** — Two lightning variants and a power button that attacks too.
27. **Spain** — First shot with three REAL balls: every one must be stopped.
28. **Germany** — Three real balls that also home in, and blocking shrinks you.
29. **Luxembourg** — Three spores at alternating heights with a punish even for blocking.
30. **Belgium** — Three carriers, two stacked ailments, and a harmful aura.
31. **Ireland** — Orbs that push you out of position before the real one arrives.
32. **Mexico** — A grab: the defender is taken out of the play entirely.
33. **Asura** — Destructive: even a successful block is punished.
34. **India** — Rain of reversing fireballs plus a grab variant.
35. **South Africa** — A telegraphed beam then a golem that throws you out of the stadium.
36. **Indonesia** — Multi-stage grab: chains hold you, the rope pulls and freezes you.
37. **Honduras** — Three balls where only one may be countered, and a hit beheads you.
38. **United Kingdom** — Ten punishing decoys that pin you, then the real arrow.
39. **Thailand** — Field-wide hazard rain before a diagonal comet.
40. **Austria** — Hazards plus the biggest ball in the game and a 5 s knockout.
41. **Switzerland** — A summoned yeti, a giant snowball and a snowman you get rolled in as.
42. **Saudi Arabia** — The one power in HS that cannot be countered.
43. **Pluto** — A screen-tall summon after a long build-up, near impossible to stop.
44. **Norway** — Critical: a random cutscene makes it faster and harder.
45. **Henos** — The final boss: a curving meteor storm that doubles on a critical.

## New engine behaviours the 45 need (beyond today's families)

- **Ailments to add:** `transform` (immobile kickable prop: jack-in-the-box, snowman, sand statue, rugby ball, kangaroo, alien, dancer),
  `vanish` (removed for N s: black hole, bottle, ashes, vine, hole), `launched` (thrown off-screen, back after N s), `buried`
  (stuck in the ground), `lifted` (hung from a web), `bubble` (floats, ball passes through), `shrink`, `grabbed` (wrapped and dragged).
- **Flight pieces:** decoy balls (unscorable fakes), sequenced volleys at set heights, homing, rise-then-dive, bobbing, hidden
  (underground / inside water / invisible), very slow, telegraphed field hazards (lightning circles, meteor rain, oil barrels),
  big-ball radius, summoned carriers (dragon, phoenix, viper, yeti, golem, titan, ship), random critical variant.
- **Punish on block:** Russia, Poland (freeze the blocker), Asura (giant fist), Luxembourg (vine), Saudi Arabia (no counter).
